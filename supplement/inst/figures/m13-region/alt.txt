Panel 1 plots candidate mean against candidate SD in observed-score units. A widening coupled .95 confidence region from 20 synthetic peers has SD limits about 7.33 and 15.48. On its right boundary, circle 1 is the lower-SD candidate and triangle 2 the upper-SD candidate. These same candidates are followed in panel 2; shading is an admissible set, not a posterior density. Panel 2 plots observed score 20 through 80 against peer CDF fraction 0 to 1. The full analytic simultaneous envelope is shaded. Dashed circle candidate 1 and dot-dash triangle candidate 2 cross at score 50 at about 0.309. Candidate 1 gives the lower envelope below 50, including score 35; candidate 2 gives it above 50, including 65. Curve coincidence is exact; the finite plotted window is not the all-score proof.
