Panel A. Four 95% interval bars on a score axis from 10 to 66 after an observed score of 35, with prior mean 50, stable variance 64 and noise variance 36. From top: credible interval for the expected score, 31.0 to 49.8; confidence interval for the expected score, 23.2 to 46.8; posterior predictive interval for the next score, 25.3 to 55.5; likelihood prediction interval, 18.4 to 51.6. Filled centers denote posterior mean 40.4; open squares denote observed score 35. A dashed vertical line marks 35 and a dot-dash line marks prior mean 50. These intervals follow one observed input; they are not sampling coverage curves. Panel B. Sampling coverage from 0 to 1 versus the fixed true expected score from 0 to 100. Confidence and likelihood prediction coincide exactly at 0.95 on one solid line. The dashed credible curve peaks near 0.99 at 50 and is about 0.36 at 20; the dotted posterior predictive curve peaks near 0.97 and is about 0.72 at 20. Markers show 20, 35 and 50. The gray prior shape is scaled for illustration, not another coverage probability. Coverage repeats the first performance and also the future performance for prediction; it does not condition on the observed score of 35.
